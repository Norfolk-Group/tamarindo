The front door

Nico onboards people himself.

New team members and investors don't fill out forms — they meet Nico.

01

Invite

You (or Nico, with your approval) send a role-tagged invitation — team or investor. Sign-up is bot-protected and passwordless.

02

Interview

First login opens a conversation. Nico asks who they are, their organization, their context with Tamarindo — and quietly builds their profile from the answers. The interview is the form.

03

Bio

Nico drafts a clean two-paragraph bio from the interview. They approve or edit it in one click.

04

NDA · gate

Nico fills the custom Tamarindo NDA with their details, renders the PDF, and collects a legally binding click-wrap signature — typed name, drawn signature, timestamp, IP and document hash on the audit trail. Signed copy delivered by email.

05

Access

Only now does the data room unlock. Confidential documents, briefs and meeting materials all check the signature — enforced in the procedures themselves, for humans and agents alike.

Accountable outward.

Nico works freely on analysis and drafts. The moment anything would leave the building, a human decides.

The approval gate

Every outbound touch — email, WhatsApp, call, calendar invite, data-room publish — flows through one procedure with a human approval step. Nico shows you the draft, the recipient, and his reasoning. You tap approve.

Terms from the record, never the model

Valuations, raise amounts and conditions render exclusively from the admin-controlled Deal Terms record. A language model never invents a number an investor sees.

Truthful presence

The status line shows what Nico is actually doing — which document he's reading, which search he's running. His animations are wired to real events, not theater. He always discloses he's an AI.

Everything on the record

Every procedure call, signature, view and download is logged. The NDA gate is enforced in code paths shared by humans and agents — there is no side door.
