# 14 — Medellín & Cartagena housing (as of 23 Aug 2026)

*Companion to 04 (ICP bands) and 07 (legal shape). This file is the
outside market brief. It is not an appraisal. Asking $/m² is not a
closed sale. Underwrite the unit.*

**How to read.** FACT = named source + date. CONTEXT = portal asking
prices, broker blogs, or derived USD. ASSUMPTION = not evidenced.

**FX (FACT).** USDCOP: ~4,918 peak Nov-2022; ~3,757 end-2025; Jul-2026
monthly avg **3,272**; mid-Aug-2026 spot **~3,090–3,150** (BanRep/CEIC;
FXStreet 18 Aug 2026; Trading Economics 14 Aug 2026). Peso **~19–22%
stronger YoY** into Aug-2026.

**FX effect (CONTEXT).** COP prices rose mid-to-high single / low-double
digits in 2025. USD asking prices rose *faster* because the peso
rallied. A US buyer who entered at 3,800–4,200 COP has large USD paper
gains even if COP was flat. Aug-2026 is an expensive USD entry vs
2023–24. USD bands below use **COP 3,200/USD** unless the source already
quotes USD.

**Not published.** Neighborhood closed-sale indices; official
days-on-market; mid-term (1–11 mo) furnished occupancy; La Galería
neighborhood tables (paywalled; cited only via Ciencuadras); Llanogrande
official $/m²; predial as % of *market* value.

Do **not** use a single USD/m² or a single “monthly rent % of value” for
underwriting. Convert at **spot TRM**, not a 2024 FX.

## Cross-cutting

**COP appreciation (FACT).** Used homes 2025: national **+9.7%**;
**Cartagena +15.7%**; **Medellín +11.0%** (Ciencuadras Informe 2025, Mar
2026, citing La Galería Inmobiliaria). New homes DANE IPVN Q1-2026 YoY:
national **+8.47%**; **Medellín AM +7.58%** (only major metro with
*negative* QoQ); **Cartagena AU +10.56%** (Observatorio Armenia reprint
of DANE, May 2026). IPVN covers new stock metro-wide — not Poblado or
Bocagrande used.

**Liquidity (CONTEXT, not official).** Liquid used apts often **2–5
months**; average **4–8**; luxury/overpriced **6–12+** (TheLatinvestor,
Jun 2026). No DANE/catastro DOM series. The 90–150 / 150–270 day figures
in 07 are still **ASSUMPTIONS**.

**HOA / administración (CONTEXT).** Not officially indexed. Typical
published examples: Poblado/Bocagrande amenity towers often **COP
0.8–2.0M+/mo**; Llanogrande parcelaciones **COP 270k–787k/mo** (listings
2026). TheLatinvestor: Bocagrande 2BR annual admin **COP 16.25M**
(~1.35M/mo) — enough to cut 5.7% gross yield to **3.4% net**.

**Predial (FACT on tariff; CONTEXT on effective).** Tax is **‰ of avalúo
catastral**, usually well below market (broker: up to ~50% below — Paul
Juan Realty, Oct 2025). Legal band Ley 44/1990: **1–16‰**. **Cartagena
2026** residential estrato 4–5–6: **6.5‰** unified (secondary digest of
Concejo tariffs; early-pay 20% before 1 Apr 2026). **Medellín:** matrix
by estrato × cadastral band (Acuerdo 066/2017 still cited); high-value
estrato 5–6 can sit **~9–15‰ of cadastral**. Effective % of *purchase
price* is lower and property-specific — do not invent a city average.
See 15 for the 2026 discount decrees.

**Estrato 5–6 utilities (FACT).** No subsidy. **+20% contribución de
solidaridad** on electricity (and analogously other utilities) funds
estrato 1–3 subsidies (Ley 142/1994; SuperServicios Concepto 028 de
2026). EPM Mar-2026: estrato 5–6 ~**1,038 COP/kWh** vs estrato 4 ~**865**
(same consumption). Cartagena warm-climate subsistence block is **173
kWh** vs Medellín **130 kWh** (Infobae 7 Apr 2026).

**Title / fiducia (FACT).** Foreigners buy on same terms as nationals.
Pull **Certificado de Tradición y Libertad** yourself (SNR). Off-plan:
funds should sit in a licensed **fiducia / fideicomiso** — protects
against developer insolvency, **not** delays, quality, or price. Register
investment with **Banco de la República** if repatriation matters.
Cartagena Centro: also heritage permits (Centro de Patrimonio);
unpermitted renovations travel with title.

## Envigado

- **$/m² (CONTEXT).** Camacol Antioquia via Mubrick (2026): avg **COP
  7.55M/m²**. El Colombiano groups Envigado with Poblado at **8.5–12M**.
  One: typically **15–25% cheaper per m²** than Poblado.
- **2–3BR USD (CONTEXT, derived).** Roughly **USD 155k–400k** for
  mainstream 70–120 m²; Benedictinos/Escobero casas higher.
- **2025 (CONTEXT).** One (citing DANE/Camacol): Envigado m² **+15% last
  year**. Camacol: Envigado prices **+15% (2025)**.
- **Rent 2–3BR unfurnished (CONTEXT).** One **Jan–Apr 2026**: Esmeraldal
  ~COP 4.0M; Antillas 4.5M; Benedictinos 5.5M; Jardines 3.4M. City apt
  rent avg **COP 4.52M** (FincaRaíz 2026). Gross yield cited **6–8%**.
- **Character (CONTEXT).** More family / school-driven; less Airbnb than
  Poblado. Envigado was **6.5%** of national digital listing share
  (Ciencuadras 2025).

## Llanogrande / Rionegro (casas campestres)

**No official neighborhood index.** Do not invent a precise COP/m².

- **Asking stock (CONTEXT).** Espacio Urbano / FincaRaíz 2026 listings
  cluster **COP 1.9–7.0B** (~USD 0.6–2.2M); FincaRaíz “fincas” average
  **~COP 4.09B**. Typical 280–400 m² built on 1,100–2,100 m² lots,
  estrato **4–6**. Tamarindo ICP-3 ($600–900k) is the lower-to-mid slice
  of that ask, not the 2M+ finca.
- **Developer claim (CONTEXT, not independent).** Conintel 27 May 2026:
  Llanogrande **COP 12–16M/m²** luxury / “from 9M”; cites **+18% annual
  2022–24** in “premium Rionegro” — treat as marketing.
- **Carry (CONTEXT).** Admin **COP 270k–787k/mo**; one listing predial
  **COP 5M/yr**. Thin rental market vs Poblado; liquidity **weaker**.
- **Risk.** Parcelación bylaws, water/septic, distance to Medellín;
  fiducia if buying sobre planos.

## Cartagena

**City (FACT, Ciencuadras Q1 2026).** Apt asking avg **COP 7.017M/m²**;
avg apt **COP 826M**; houses **COP 1.747B**; apt rent avg **COP 4.2M**,
Historic + Caribe Norte up to **COP 5.8M**. Used 2025 **+15.7% COP**.

**TuLugar asking 26 Jul 2026 (FACT as scrape).** Bocagrande n=580, median
**USD 380k / $3,323/m²**, typical 2BR **USD 274k**; Castillogrande n=281,
median **USD 581k / $3,234/m²**; Centro n=153, median **USD 1.5M /
$5,406/m²** (casa-heavy).

### Bocagrande

- **AMBQ Observatorio (offers Feb–Jun 2025, FACT).** Classic stock
  **150–650 m², avg ~430 m²**. Offer median **COP 3.10B**; mean **COP
  4.52B**. $/m² median **COP 13.95M**, mean **13.78M**, range
  **~6.0–23.4M**. Older 2nd-line **COP 4.5–6.5M/m²**; newer 1st-line
  **9–13M**.
- **2–3BR USD (CONTEXT).** New compact tourist 2BR often **USD
  250–450k** (TuLugar). Classic 150 m²+ family apts **USD 0.7–1.5M+**.
  Do not mix the two. Tamarindo ICP-2 ($500–800k) is above the compact
  tourist 2BR and below the classic family tower.
- **Rent / STR.** LTR Bocagrande 2BR yield **5.7% gross / 3.4% net**
  (TheLatinvestor 2026). STR city: AirROI Jun-2025–May-2026 **38.4% occ,
  ADR USD 165**; Airbtics Feb-2025–Jan-2026 **56% occ, USD 18k/yr, 8,820
  listings (+29% YoY, occ −5.1%)**. High season **Dec–Mar** (Jan peak);
  trough **May / Sep–Oct**.

### Castillo Grande

- **AMBQ H1 2025 (FACT).** Offer median **COP 2.85B**, mean **COP
  2.90B**, avg **296 m²**; $/m² median **COP 10.04M**, mean **10.75M**,
  range **~5.7–20.2M**.
- More residential / larger units than Bocagrande tourist towers;
  thinner STR (Airbtics hotspot count **61** vs Bocagrande 326). Yields
  compress because prices are high (TheLatinvestor 4.5–6% LTR in premium
  waterfront).

### Old City / Centro Histórico (incl. San Diego)

- **No AMBQ bulletin** (H1 2025 coastal-only). Portal $/m² **USD
  ~3,900–6,300** (TheLatinvestor 2026) / TuLugar Centro **$5,406/m²**.
  Stock = restored casas + small apts — **not comparable** to Bocagrande
  towers.
- Heritage + humidity + night noise; STR license + RNT + *propiedad
  horizontal* bylaws. Finite inventory → sticky asking prices;
  **maintenance is the tax**.

**Cartagena risks (FACT/CONTEXT).** Tourism seasonality (cash-flow gap
4–6 months). Salt/humidity opex. Title/POA fraud and hidden
admin/predial debt (TheLatinvestor 2026). Off-plan towers without real
fiducia. UNESCO/heritage constraints. STR listings **+109% over 3Y**
while occupancy fell — supply grew faster than nights.
