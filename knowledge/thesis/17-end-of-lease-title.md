# 17 — How the lessee buys the home or auto (as of 24 Aug 2026)

*Companion to 13 (deal waterfall), 16 (characterization), and 18 (KYC).
This is CONTEXT for counsel, not a legal opinion. Paying the US balloon
does **not** by itself put the client on the Colombian folio.*

**ASSUMED stack:** Intervest vehicle holds title via Colombian sucursal
or fiducia. Client has Colombian *comodato* (gratuitous use) and a
US-law lease with a purchase option floored at **20% of asset cost**.
Client may prepay. Launch is **individuals (SSNs)**, not companies
(Granola). The purchase right is **assignable, subject to local KYC**.

## Two-contract stack

**FACT.** *Comodato* (CC 2200 ff.) is a loan for use. It does **not**
transfer *dominio*. *Comodato precario* can be called at any time (CC
2219).

**FACT.** Sale of land is not perfect until *escritura pública* (CC
1857). *Tradición* of land is by inscription at the Oficina de Registro
de Instrumentos Públicos (CC 756; Ley 1579/2012).

**CONTEXT.** The US balloon closes the **payment** contract. The
Colombian *escritura* + registro (home) or RUNT *traspaso* (auto) closes
**title**. They are independent closings.

## Homes — exercise sequence

1. **ASSUMPTION.** Client gives option notice under the US lease.
2. **ASSUMPTION.** Client wires the balloon to Intervest in the US.
3. **CONTEXT.** Title study: *Certificado de Tradición y Libertad*,
   *paz y salvo* predial, *valorización* if any, no blocking embargo.
4. **FACT.** *Escritura pública* of *compraventa* (or fiducia
   equivalent) at a Colombian notary (D.L. 960/1970). Grantor =
   sucursal legal rep or fiduciaria. Grantee = the client (or a SAS /
   US LLC if the option names them).
5. **FACT.** Inscribe at the ORIP of the *predio*. Ownership vs third
   parties dates from **registro**, not the notary signing (CC 756).
6. **FACT.** Non-resident using foreign-source funds: canalize COP
   through an IMC and register as FDI (BanRep DCIP-83 / RE 1/2018).
7. **CONTEXT.** Terminate *comodato* in writing. Close the US lease
   per its exercise clause.

**Fiducia vs sucursal (CONTEXT).** If title sits in a *patrimonio
autónomo*, the instrument may be sale by the fiduciaria, *restitución*,
or assignment of fiduciary rights — not a simple sucursal deed.

### Who appears / poder

**FACT.** The notary verifies identity and sufficiency of any mandate
(D.L. 960/1970).

**CONTEXT.** Buyer in person: passport. Buyer abroad: *poder especial*
naming the *matrícula inmobiliaria*, price, and the act. Colombian
consulate → no apostille. US notary → apostille + Spanish translation.
US LLC buyer: apostilled formation + authority + NIT/RUT. Spouse may
need to appear depending on marital regime.

### Foreigners and non-residents

| Item | Need? | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Yes | FACT (notarial ID) |
| Visa / residency | **No** for title | FACT — no visa statute conditions land purchase |
| *Cédula de extranjería* | Only if they already have one | CONTEXT |
| RUT / NIT | Yes in practice (DIAN) | FACT — non-resident investors register RUT |
| US SSN/ITIN | Not a Colombian title document | CONTEXT |
| Apostille | On foreign *poder* / corporate papers | FACT |
| Source of funds | Notary SIPLAFT / UIAF | FACT — notaries report passport foreigners (UIAF IA 17, 2016) |

**FACT.** A tourist can buy. A Tamarindo lease is **not** an
inversionista visa (title is the vehicle’s until the option — thesis
15). Private urban lots are generally acquirable; the 50 km / 2 km
foreign-ban blog rule is **not** authority (thesis 16).

**FACT.** Isolated real-estate purchase by a foreign company is not, by
itself, “permanent business” requiring a *sucursal* (C. Co. 471). A
Colombian SAS is operationally simpler.

### BanRep / withholding

**FACT.** FX for international investment must go through the *mercado
cambiario*. The *declaración de cambio* serves as registration when
funds are canalized (DCIP-83 ch. 7). Failure mode is later
**repatriation**, not ORIP inscription.

**ASSUMPTION.** Balloon paid in the US still needs a Colombian FX/FDI
story. Counsel designs the wires.

**FACT.** ET 398: 1% withholding on a **natural person’s** sale of
fixed assets. ET 401 parágrafo: if the **buyer is a legal person**,
renta withholding is a precondition of the *escritura* (DIAN Concepto
5148/2026). Housing rates: **1%** then **2.5%** (DURT 1.2.4.9.1).

**CONTEXT.** Individual buyer → sucursal seller: usually **no**
notarial retefuente (seller is not a natural person; 401 is keyed to a
legal-person buyer). SAS/LLC buyer → sucursal: **buyer** withholds
unless seller is *autorretenedor*.

**CONTEXT.** Notary + registro often **~2–3%** of price excluding
retefuente. Impuesto de registro is departmental. Point to thesis 15
for the buyer closing stack.

### Walk-away and early buyout

**FACT.** Option not exercised ≠ forced sale. Title stays on the
vehicle. *Comodato* ends; possession returns.

**ASSUMPTION (Tamarindo / Granola).** No Colombian put. Client can
**prepay any time with no penalty**; pay residual + remaining
principal. Colombian closing is the same *escritura* + registro, just
earlier. Lease **extension** was deferred as too complex.

**CONTEXT (Granola, still open).** Tax counsel on *ganancia ocasional*
at the transfer; Colombian KYC pack for the final step.

## Autos

**FACT.** Title lives in **RUNT** + the *organismo de tránsito* of
*matrícula*. Buyer and seller must be inscribed in RUNT before
*traspaso*. Foreign adult ID: *cédula de extranjería*, **valid
passport**, or PPT.

**FACT.** A non-resident individual **can** hold vehicle title on a
passport. A Colombian company / sucursal is **not** required.

**FACT.** Permanent import of **used** passenger vehicles is not
authorized (Andean automotive rules). Tourist temporary import (typically
up to 6 months) is **not** a path to Colombian *matrícula*. Tamarindo
end-of-term path is **local** *traspaso* or dealer *matrícula inicial*.

### Traspaso (used)

**FACT (national list; Bogotá VUS / typical organismo):** both parties
on RUNT; SOAT and *revisión técnico-mecánica* loaded when applicable;
*paz y salvo* *impuesto de vehículo* and SIMIT *comparendos*;
formulario + *contrato de compraventa* + VIN; retefuente receipt where
required; *derechos de tránsito* at the organismo of *matrícula*.
Plates usually **stay with the vehicle**. After **6 Feb 2026**, only
named-owner transfers (no “persona indeterminada”).

**FACT.** ET 398: 1% if the **seller is a natural person**. If the
seller is the *sucursal*, confirm whether the secretaría still collects
1% or treats the company as *autorretenedor*. Annual *impuesto de
vehículo* is a transfer **condition**, not a transfer tax.
