# 13 — One Deal, Start to Finish: ICP-1 at Current Policy

*The typical deal Nico should walk anyone through. ICP-1 "Poblado
Executive": $420k El Poblado apartment, 120 months. Current policy
(2026-08-23): base 11.5% + FICO blend ≈ **11.84% effective**, balloon
**20% of asset**, 40% down. All numbers from the engine; every input is
a model variable.*

## The shape

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | $420,000 (seller paid in full day 0) |
| Client down payment (40%) | $168,000 |
| Vehicle funds (60% LTV) | $252,000 |
| Monthly lease, 120 months | **$3,223** |
| Purchase option at month 120 | **$84,000** (20% of asset — true-lease floor, thesis 07) |
| Effective client rate | 11.84% (11.5% base + FICO tier blend) |

## Day 0 — closing

- Client wires $168k down; vehicle draws $252k; seller gets $420k.
- Title goes to the vehicle's **sucursal**. Client signs the **US-law
  lease + comodato** with purchase option. It is a US deal — the client
  is buying money in the US from Intervest's vehicle; Intervest buys the
  asset in Colombia — until the option is exercised.
- Fees at close: activation $5,040 (2% of draw) and origination $2,520
  to Tamarindo US; insurance commission $1,008; Colombia closing $2,200 +
  inspection $400 to the sucursal; US pays the sucursal a $1,000 mandate.

## Months 1–120

Client pays $3,223/mo. Each payment splits into principal (amortizing to
the $84k balloon) and interest on the declining balance. Lifetime
interest ≈ **$218.8k**. Of it: ~80% to the vehicle, **20% ($43.8k) to
Tamarindo**, plus servicing 75 bps/yr (**$13.9k** life). Client also pays
$120/mo Colombia admin. When the unit is in the rental pool (each ICP has
its own % of time rented, default 30%), Tamarindo takes its rental share:
**$254/mo when the unit is occupied ≈ $76/mo averaged** across the year on
ICP-1.

## Month 120

Client pays the $84,000 option. Vehicle balance → 0. **US payment is not
Colombian title:** ownership on the folio still needs *escritura* + ORIP
registro (homes) or RUNT *traspaso* (autos). Visa is not required;
passport + RUT is. Walk-away leaves title with the vehicle. See thesis
**17** (mechanics) and **18** (KYC).

## Who makes what over the life (engine math)

| Party | Puts in | Takes out |
|---|---|---|
| Client | ~$664k over 10 years | The $420k home, owned |
| Intervest vehicle | $252k day 0 | **$413k back ≈ 9.08% IRR** — inside the 8.5–11.5% band |
| Tamarindo US | underwriting + servicing desks | **~$75.3k** (activation, origination, spread, servicing, insurance, rental share) |
| Colombia sucursal | closings, field, liaison | ~$17k client fees + $1k mandate |
| Seller | the apartment | $420k, day 0 |

## Why these numbers are shaped this way

- **Balloon 20% of asset** — Rev. Proc. 2001-28 residual guideline and
  UCC 1-203 non-nominal option; the lease must never read as a mortgage
  (thesis 07).
- **11.84% blended** — FICO-tiered (750–779 +75 bps / 780–809 base /
  810+ −25 bps), "close to hard money (10–16%) but not quite" per
  Ricardo; Intervest sets rate policy (thesis 12).
- **Vehicle 9.08%** — fixed from 8.35% (below band) by the 50 bps base
  step-up + tier blend, without cutting Tamarindo's 20% strip.
