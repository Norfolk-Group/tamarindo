*How Tamarindo US pays salaries. 2026-08-23. Ownership names are TBD.*

## Two pots of money

- **Intervest / other vehicles** fund **leases** (homes, autos, aircraft). That
  cash is not OpCo working capital. It does not pay Dov, Rosario, Ricardo,
  or Tom.
- **Equity** funds the **venture** — payroll, desks, WhatsApp, legal paper,
  marketing, sales, accounting. New shareholders can be anyone. Intervest is
  not on this cap table unless a later term sheet says so.
