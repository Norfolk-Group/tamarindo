# 15 — Living in Medellín & Cartagena (as of 23 Aug 2026)

*Practical facts for a US person who owns or leases a home and visits
part-year. Companion to 14 (market) and 07 (lease/comodato shape). Not
immigration, tax, or employment advice.*

**How to read.** FACT = named source + date. CONTEXT = secondary tables
or mid-2026 order of magnitude. ASSUMPTION = unpublished or inferred.

**FX.** 14 uses Aug-2026 spot **~COP 3,090–3,150** and converts some
bands at **3,200**. USD school/help bands below that used **~3,800** as
a mid-2026 order of magnitude — do not mix the two without saying so.
Always convert at **spot TRM**.

A lease or a deed does **not** confer a visa, cédula, work rights, a
waiver of the tourist-day cap, tax residency, RNT/Airbnb rights, or PH
permission for short stays.

## Visas / stay

**FACT — Tourist (not a visa).** US citizens enter visa-free. Migración
issues a **PIP / Permiso de Turismo (PT)** up to **90 calendar days**.
Extend via **PTP (prórroga)** before expiry, typically another 90, **cap
180 days in the same calendar year**. PTP fee on Migración’s 2026 table:
**COP 150,000**. Apply ≥5 business days before expiry. Overstay → multa;
may block exit until paid. Check-Mig 1–72h before flight. Return ticket
often required.

- https://portal.migracioncolombia.gov.co/tramites-y-servicios/tramites-generales/prorroga/permiso-temporal-de-permanencia-ptp-/-prorroga
- https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/colombia.html

**FACT — Current names (Res. 5477/2022, still the frame in 2026).**
Cancillería types: **V (visitante), M (migrante), R (residente)**.

| Track | Type | Core 2026 money test | Max grant | Work? | Path to R? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pensionado | **M** | Lifetime pensión ≥ **3 SMMLV** = **COP 5,252,715/mo** (~USD 1,380 at 3,800) | 3 yrs | No | Yes, after 5 yrs as titular |
| Rentista | **V** (not M) | Periodic renta ≥ **10 SMMLV** = **COP 17,509,050/mo** | 2 yrs | No | **No** |
| Inversionista inmueble | **M** | Property **solely in name**, ≥ **350 SMMLV** = **COP 612,816,750** (~USD 161k at 3,800) **plus** Banco de la República FDI extract | 3 yrs | No | Yes, 5 yrs |
| IED (empresa) | **M** | FDI ≥ **650 SMMLV** = **COP 1,138,088,250** | 3 yrs | — | Yes, 5 yrs |
| Nómada digital | **V** | Foreign remote income ≥ 3 SMMLV | typically 2 yrs | Remote only | **No** |

SMMLV 2026 = **COP 1,750,905** (Decreto 1469/2025; Consejo de Estado
suspended it Feb 2026, then **Decreto 159/2026** kept the **same** figure
provisionally). Visa COP floors move if SMMLV is later changed.

A tourist **can** buy. **M inversionista** needs **350 SMMLV + FDI
registration**, not “I own something.” A Tamarindo lease never qualifies
as the inversionista property (title is the vehicle’s until the option).

**FACT — Cédula de extranjería vs passport.** Cédula = in-country ID for
visa titular/beneficiario **>3 months**, age ≥7. Register visa / start
cédula within **15 calendar days** of entry (or of in-country issuance).
2026 fee **COP 294,000**. Passport remains the **travel** document.
Tourists use passport only.

- https://portal.migracioncolombia.gov.co/tramites-y-servicios/tramites-generales/cedula-de-extranjeria

## Taxes (US person + Colombian property)

**FACT — Predial.** Annual municipal tax on **avalúo catastral**, not
asking price. National band **1–16‰** (Ley 44/1990). Catastral is
supposed to sit **60–100% of commercial** (Ley 14/1983 family); after
updates it can be close to market, otherwise often well below. 2026
national reajuste of non-updated avalúos: **+3%** (DNP Decreto
1480/2025).

**CONTEXT — Effective rates (secondary tables; confirm on the receipt):**

| | Residencial estrato 5–6 | Early-pay 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **Medellín** | **9.5‰ of catastral** (~0.95%) | **5%** if paid in Q1 (Decreto 080/2026) |
| **Cartagena** | **6.5‰ unified** for estratos 4–6 (~0.65%) | **20%** if paz y salvo, pay by ~1 Apr 2026; then 10% window |

If catastral is ~70% of market: Medellín ≈ **0.67% of commercial**,
Cartagena ≈ **0.46%**. Pay annually; many municipios allow **cuotas**.
Portals: GeoMedellín / https://portaltributario.cartagena.gov.co

**FACT — 183-day tax residency (current).** Estatuto Tributario **art.
10**: >**183 calendar days** (continuous or not, **entry+exit count**)
in **any 365-day window**. If the window spans two years, residency
starts in the **second** year. **Visa is irrelevant.** Residents:
worldwide income + DIAN return. Non-residents: Colombian-source only
(rents, Colombian gains).

**FACT — Rental withholding.** Inmueble arriendo: **3.5% retención** when
the payer is an agente retenedor and the canon ≥ the UVT floor (**10 UVT
= COP 523,740** under Decreto 572/2025 / 2026 UVT **COP 52,374**). Credit
against renta.

**FACT — US.** Citizens/green-card holders file **worldwide** income.
**FIRPTA (IRC 897/1445) does not apply** to Colombian real estate (only
USRPIs). Rent the unit → **Schedule E**; expenses/depreciation allowed
under US rules. FTC **Form 1116**; FEIE is **earned** income only (not
rent/pensión). FBAR/FATCA if accounts qualify. Colombian banks may
report under the **FATCA IGA** (2015) and the **2001 TIEA**.

**FACT — No income-tax treaty in force.** IRS treaty A–Z / Table 3
(updated through **26 Sep 2025**) has **no Colombia DTA**. No
tie-breaker, no treaty pension/dividend rates. Relief = domestic FTC /
Colombian crédito. TIEA + FATCA = **information**, not double-tax
relief. No totalization agreement (CONTEXT, practitioner guides 2026).

- https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z

**FACT — Short-stay platforms.** Tourism lodging needs **RNT**
(Confecámaras) + **TRA** guest register + PH reglamento that **allows**
vivienda turística. IVA **19%** if the host is **responsable de IVA**;
many small personas naturales are **no responsables** (ET art. 437
tests). Platforms also charge IVA on **their** service fee. Contribución
parafiscal turismo **0.25%** where lodging rules apply.

**ASSUMPTION — Buyer closing stack (~% of escritura).** Broker/notary
calculators 2026, **not** a statute dump: notaría **0.54%** split 50/50
(+ IVA 19% on derechos); **registro + beneficencia ~1.0–2.0%** (buyer;
dept-specific); **retención 1% / 2.5%** is the **seller’s** advance.
Buyer all-in often **~2–4%**. Impuesto al consumo 2% / timbre only on
high UVT deals. Confirm Antioquia vs Bolívar tarifas at the notaría.

- https://www.metrocuadrado.com/noticias/guia-de-compra/gastos-notariales-de-venta-y-compra-del-inmueble-en-2026-4709

## Travel & transportation

**FACT — Airports.** International Medellín = **MDE José María Córdova,
Rionegro** (not the valley). City strip **EOH Olaya Herrera** = mostly
domestic. Cartagena = **CTG Rafael Núñez, Crespo**.

**FACT — Distances / drive (light traffic; rain/rush adds a lot).**

| Leg | Distance | Time |
|---|---|---|
| MDE → El Poblado | ~30–35 km via **Túnel de Oriente** | **35–50 min** (75+ in peak) |
| MDE / Rionegro → **Llanogrande** | adjacent Oriente | **10–20 min** |
| Poblado → Envigado | metro-adjacent | **10–25 min** |
| Poblado → Llanogrande | Túnel | **35–60 min** |
| CTG → Centro / Getsemaní | ~3 km | **10–20 min** |
| CTG → Bocagrande | ~5–10 km | **15–25 min** (Malecón traffic) |

**As of Aug 2026 — US nonstops (schedules churn).** MDE: **MIA** AA+AV
(~daily, ~3/day combined); **JFK** AV; **IAH** UA daily; **FLL** B6+AV
(+Spirit on some boards). CTG: **MIA** AA+AV (~8/wk on one aggregator).
**UA IAH & IAD–CTG** announced from **17 Dec 2026**, 4×/wk. Always
re-check.

**Domestic MDE–CTG:** daily; Avianca, LATAM, JetSMART, Wingo;
**~1h05–1h20**.

**In-city.** Medellín: **Metro + Metroplús + cables/tranvía** — reliable
valley backbone; **no Metro to MDE**. Taxis yellow/official. **Uber /
DiDi / inDrive** widely used; **not a licensed taxi substitute** —
airport pickup is off-curb (garage/departures); Rionegro has fined app
drivers. Cartagena: **zone-fare official taxis** (airport booth ticket);
Centro streets are slow, one-way, pedestrian-heavy — walking + taxi
beats a car.

## Home maintenance (estrato 5–6 apt vs casa campestre)

**FACT — Administración (Ley 675 PH).** Assembly-set budget; **no legal
IPC cap**. 2026 increases often **12–25%** where 24h portería/aseo is
indexed to the 23% SMMLV jump (CONTEXT, Lonja/Caracol). Usually covers:
common aseo, portería/CCTV, elevators, common agua/energía, zonas
húmedas, building RC/incendio. **Does not cover:** unit interior,
private utilities, predial, interior AC, your contents.

**Casa campestre:** often no PH (or thin rural conjunto). Owner pays
jardín, pool, pozo/septic, planta, private security.

**FACT — Utilities / estrato.** Estratos **1–3 subsidized**; **4** ≈
cost; **5–6 pay full + contribución de solidaridad** (water/aseo
commonly **+50% / +60%** on the reference; energy/gas also contribute).
Gas, agua, energía billed to the unit.

**FACT — Domestic help 2026.** Legal floor **por días** (8h): **COP
76,394/day** (~USD 20 at 3,800) = SMMLV/30 + auxilio + Sunday fraction
(Ley **2466/2025** + SMMLV). Written contract, **PILA**
(salud/pensión/ARL/caja), primas/cesantías pro rata. **ASSUMPTION
market** estrato 5–6 Poblado/Bocagrande: **COP 80–150k/day** or **USD
400–900/mo** for 2–3 days/week.

- https://www.infobae.com/colombia/2026/03/19/empleadas-domesticas-por-dias-el-minimo-que-deben-recibir-en-2026-y-los-nuevos-derechos-que-los-empleadores-estan-obligados-a-cumplir/

**CONTEXT — Recurring extras.** Pool/jardín service, fumigación
(termites + dengue), AC **essential in Cartagena** (salt + humidity →
coils/filters every 1–3 mo). Medellín rain peaks **Apr–May, Sep–Nov**
(mold, roof, drains). Cartagena is humid year-round.

**FACT — Predial cadence.** Once a year; discounts for lump-sum early
pay; otherwise cuotas on the municipal calendar.

**CONTEXT — Insurance.** Colombian **hogar / todo riesgo hogar**
(edificio + contenidos + RC) is what follows the asset. A **US
lessor/HO-3/HO-4 does not** cover a Colombia house. PH building policy ≠
your contents or loss-of-rent. **ASSUMPTION** premium mid/high apt:
**COP 1–4M/yr**.

**Building vs owner.** PH: comunes + fachada rules. Owner: interiors,
fixtures, leaks originating in the unit, predial, private contracts.

**ASSUMPTION — Furnished mid-term / rental-pool (Ashoka-style).**
Turnover (linen, deep clean, restock): **COP 150–400k** per stay; more
after long vacancy or Cartagena AC/mold remediation. Budget **1 extra
clean + linen set per turnover**, plus a local operator if you are not
in-country.
