step-up as the base case (2026-08-23).*

## Why this size is defensible for Intervest

- **FACT — Intervest public site:** they fund *direct structured finance
  on physical assets under leases or rental agreements* and private
  credit into specialty-finance platforms. They report 23 platform
  investments, $21.3B+ annual originations, $25B+ across 160+ vehicles
  ([Specialty Finance](https://intervest.com/specialty-finance/),
  [home](https://intervest.com/), accessed 2026-08-23).
- **FACT — 2026 activity:** an Intervest affiliate acquired Kapitus
  (SMB specialty finance) and closed a Twain bridge-lending JV
  ([PR Newswire Kapitus](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/intervest-capital-partners-acquires-kapitus-302834859.html);
  [Twain JV](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/twain-capital-partners-and-intervest-capital-partners-announce-joint-venture-partnership-to-scale-twains-bridge-lending-platform-302691729.html)).
  They buy and scale **platforms**, not one-off loans.
- **FACT — internal:** $10M test + $10M on KPIs; no exclusivity, ROFR
  only; conversations also sketched a **$50–100M** scale raise. A $75M
  Intervest line at year 10 sits in that band.
- **OPINION — pitch, not religion:** Intervest underwrites **endpoint
  control**. Tamarindo is an AI-first fintech that owns the US credit
  box and the Colombia close (sucursal, notary, comodato, WhatsApp
  servicing). That is the reason a $25B manager would stay at $20M
  until KPIs print, then walk toward $75M — not because a spreadsheet
  compounds 20% every six months.

follows the year-10 goal, not an unbounded home ramp.
